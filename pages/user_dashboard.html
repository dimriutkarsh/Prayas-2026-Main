Process flow

How duplicate detection works

From five separate reports to one prioritised issue, without ever deleting or merging a single complaint.

01

Citizens report an issue

Different citizens in the same area submit complaints. They describe the same road damage in different words, on WhatsApp or the web.

02

The system compares them

Every pair is scored on three signals: matching category, matching location, and shared keywords such as “potholes” or “school road”.

03

Reports above 70% group up

Pairs scoring above the threshold form one issue group such as GRP-001. Individual complaints are never deleted or merged; every citizen keeps their own ticket.

04

Officials fix one issue

The department sees one grouped issue affecting three citizens instead of three tickets, and one update reaches every reporter at once.
